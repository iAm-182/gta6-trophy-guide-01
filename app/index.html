    Roadmap to the platinum

    The order that wastes the least time. Story first — the unmissable trophies come free as you play — then strangers, activities, collectibles and money. Do the missable ones when they appear; everything else can be cleaned up after the credits.

    Missable trophies — do them when they come up

    These 8 are flagged from the official descriptions: each one describes a specific moment, a partner who is only available in certain story phases, or a one-time retry. The flags are our reading of the descriptions, not confirmed in-game yet — the game only released today.

    About the story missions: the named story-mission trophies (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) are listed as unmissable on the assumption missions can be replayed, as in GTA V. If GTA 6 has no mission replay, treat every story mission as missable too.

      About & sources

      What this is

      An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI, released 19 November 2026. Tick trophies off as you earn them; your progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back.

      How to use it

      1. Play the story (Roadmap, stage 1) and tick trophies off as they pop.
      2. When a missable moment comes up, open its guide first — the missable flags are on the Missables tab.
      3. After the credits, work stages 2–5 in order; the checklist's counters (e.g. 0/250 stunts) track the big grinds.
      4. Use Hide story spoilers to keep story trophy names and descriptions hidden until you earn them.

      Offline & private

      This app is static files only. It loads nothing from the network, sends nothing anywhere, and your progress never leaves this browser unless you export it yourself.
